Questions, answered.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan a QR code, take a selfie in their mobile browser and get their gallery instantly.

How does face matching work?

Glimpse compares a guest’s selfie with the faces in the event gallery and returns only their photos.

Is biometric data private?

Yes. Face data is encrypted, never shared and can be deleted at any time.

How fast do photos appear?

Photos are matched as the photographer uploads, so new shots show up within moments.

Can we show a live slideshow at the venue?

Yes. Cast the Live TV slideshow to any screen and photos appear as they arrive.
